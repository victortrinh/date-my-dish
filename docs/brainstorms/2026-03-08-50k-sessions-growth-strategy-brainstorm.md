# 50K Sessions/Month Growth Strategy

**Date**: 2026-03-08
**Status**: Decided
**Context**: Site has 16 content pieces (11 recipes, 5 articles), near-zero organic traffic, Pinterest recently fixed, 1 recipe + 1 article/week publishing pace.

## What We're Building

A hybrid growth strategy combining **Pinterest for quick traffic wins** and **SEO for durable compounding traffic**, with a distinctive **cheeky, confident brand voice** as the differentiator. Target: 50k sessions/month within 12-18 months.

## Why This Approach

- **Pinterest is the fastest free traffic source for food blogs** — pins have a long shelf life (months vs hours for social posts) and food is Pinterest's #1 category
- **SEO compounds over time** — once recipes rank, they drive traffic for years with zero effort
- **Brand voice is the moat** — with only 16 posts, we can't compete on volume. A memorable, flirty-but-not-pretentious personality makes content shareable and builds a following
- **All free tools** — no paid tools initially; invest only when ROI is proven

## Key Decisions

### 1. Brand Voice: "Cheeky & Confident" (PG-13)
- Double entendres welcome, playful food-romance metaphors
- Like a fun date conversation — confident without cringe
- Food is the star, personality is the seasoning
- Applies to: recipe prose, pin titles/descriptions, meta descriptions, newsletter, FAQs
- Example pin title: "This Pasta Will Get You a Second Date" vs "Penne alla Vodka Recipe"

### 2. Pinterest Multi-Pin Automation (3 pins per recipe)
- **Pin 1** (on deploy): Hero image + recipe title — already working
- **Pin 2** (3-7 days later): Alternate title angle + cheeky description (auto-generated from frontmatter at publish time)
- **Pin 3** (7-14 days later): Seasonal/occasion angle (auto-generated)
- Title variations stored in frontmatter or a data file, generated by Claude from recipe keywords/occasion/cuisine
- Extend existing `social-post-on-deploy.yml` + add a scheduled "pin rotation" action

### 3. Priority Fundamentals (Free, Implement in Order)

| Priority | Action | Why | Effort |
|----------|--------|-----|--------|
| 1 | Cloudflare Web Analytics | Can't optimize what you can't measure | Small |
| 2 | AggregateRating schema (star ratings) | Rich snippets in Google = higher CTR | Medium |
| 3 | Multi-pin automation | 3x Pinterest surface area per recipe | Medium |
| 4 | Topical clusters + internal linking | Group recipes by cuisine/occasion, cross-link | Medium |
| 5 | Newsletter capture (Kit free tier) | Build owned audience, not dependent on algorithms | Medium |
| 6 | Brand voice refresh on existing content | Rewrite prose/descriptions with cheeky voice | Ongoing |

### 4. Content Strategy: Low-Competition + Niche
- Use free tools (Google Keyword Planner, AlsoAsked, Google autocomplete) to find low-difficulty recipe keywords
- Prioritize recipes that reinforce the date-night niche and build topical clusters
- Target long-tail keywords (your quinoa salmon already ranks #3 for a long-tail — proof it works)
- Each new recipe should link to 2-3 related existing recipes

### 5. Realistic Traffic Projections

| Month | Sessions/mo | Primary Source |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| 1-3 | 500-2,000 | Pinterest + direct |
| 4-6 | 2,000-8,000 | Pinterest growing + first organic rankings |
| 7-9 | 8,000-20,000 | SEO compounding + Pinterest steady |
| 10-12 | 15,000-35,000 | SEO dominant + Pinterest + newsletter |
| 13-18 | 30,000-50,000+ | Compounding across all channels |

*These assume consistent 1 recipe + 1 article/week and steady Pinterest pinning.*

## What We're NOT Doing (Yet)
- **Paid ads** — revisit when we have data on which recipes convert
- **Video/Reels** — nice-to-have but not needed for 50k sessions
- **Guest posting/backlink outreach** — focus on content + Pinterest first
- **Increasing content pace** — quality at current pace > volume
- **Comments system** — low priority for traffic growth
- **Recipe scaling/bookmarks** — nice UX features but don't drive traffic
