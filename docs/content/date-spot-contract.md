# Date Spot content contract

Issue #497 implements the model agreed in #496 and PR #507. `src/content/date-spots.json` is an array of Date Spots, consumed by the Astro `dateSpots` collection. It starts empty deliberately: existing reviews require fresh visits and human re-reporting, not score conversion. Public rendering, retirement of legacy collections, and Notion workflow changes belong to the other pivot tickets.

Each record is one atomic English (`en`) and Canadian French (`fr-CA`) Locale Pair. Shared identity, Spot Type, qualitative verdict, byline, photographic provenance, payment arrangement, and freshness dates are stored once. Each locale supplies its own slug, title, metadata, source/fact notes, image description/credit, disclosure, essentials, reporting modules, and dated material updates. The collection ID identifies the pair; routes should use the locale's slug. Publication uses the shared `freshness.published` date. There is no single-language publication mode.

The executable source of truth is `src/content-contracts/date-spot.mjs`. All objects are strict: unknown fields, including historical numeric ratings, are errors rather than silently discarded. Restaurant and Bar require a qualitative Review Verdict and reasoned Good-for Signals from the six agreed occasions, plus full review modules and a signed Reporter's Note. Restaurant requires cuisine and ordering guidance; Bar food reporting is optional. Activity and Chef-led Experience require planning modules and cannot carry review verdicts or review modules. Chef-led Experience additionally requires a named chef or bartender host. Pairing links are optional when earned by reporting.

`npm run validate:source` and the build's `prebuild` hook validate the whole array, including unique IDs/slugs, complete locale pairs, matching Good-for assessments and material-update dates, calendar dates/chronology, and the presence of the photograph under `public/images/date-spots/`. Astro also validates individual records using the same schema. Consumers must render the Freshness Record, disclosure, and reasons; schema validation alone cannot prove they are visible.

`npm run test:contracts` exercises all four variants using explicit `[TEST ONLY]` tokens outside the content collection. These values are mechanical test data, not reporting, human-authored translations, or publishable fixtures. No editorial content or photographs are generated by this implementation.

Before adding a real record, Victor must supply both complete human-authored versions and a genuine DMD-held photograph. The `authorship` and image `provenance` fields are required editorial attestations, not automated proof of origin. Human review must verify truthful reasons, accurate alt text, original reporting, appropriate occasion selection, and that an Activity does not duplicate a Restaurant or Bar. Validation cannot infer those facts from prose.

Contributor Recipes and Extended Profiles use parallel empty collections, `src/content/contributor-recipes.json` and `src/content/extended-profiles.json`, with their own executable contracts and Notion Story templates. They remain optional companions: a recipe must be supplied by an identified chef or bartender with a documented source; a profile must document original interview reporting and a companion Date Spot. See `notion/templates/` for their type-specific publication checklists.
