# Date Spot content contract

Issue #497 implements the model agreed in #496 and PR #507. `src/content/date-spots.json` is an array of Date Spots, consumed by the Astro `dateSpots` collection. It starts empty deliberately: existing reviews require fresh visits and human re-reporting, not score conversion. Public rendering, retirement of legacy collections, and Notion workflow changes belong to the other pivot tickets.

Each record is one atomic English (`en`) and Canadian French (`fr-CA`) Locale Pair. Shared identity, Spot Type, category, qualitative verdict, price range, byline, photographs (the Editorial Image plus keyed `photos`), payment arrangement, Instagram handle, booking URL, dated Google review snapshots, and freshness dates are stored once. Each locale supplies its own slug, title, metadata, source/fact notes, image description/credit, disclosure, essentials, reporting modules, and dated material updates. The collection ID identifies the pair; routes should use the locale's slug. Publication uses the shared `freshness.published` date. There is no single-language publication mode.

The executable source of truth is `src/content-contracts/date-spot.mjs`. All objects are strict: unknown fields, including historical numeric ratings, are errors rather than silently discarded. Every Date Spot carries the three-state verdict (`favourite`, `conditional`, `pass`). Restaurant and Bar require a verdict headline and reason, 4 to 6 reasoned Good-for Signals from the six agreed occasions, structured drinks, an itemised real cost, a signed My note with visit count, Before you book questions, and at least 1,000 reader-facing words per locale; their meta title leads with `{Name}, {Neighbourhood}` and their meta description runs 150 to 160 characters. Restaurant requires cuisine and 4 to 7 tagged dish cards; Bar food reporting is optional. Activity and Chef-led Experience require a category, When it works rows, and planning modules, and cannot carry review modules. Chef-led Experience additionally requires a named chef or bartender host. See `docs/editorial-publishing-system.md` for the page each field feeds.

`npm run validate:source` and the build's `prebuild` hook validate the whole array, including unique IDs/slugs, complete locale pairs, a Locale Pair that agrees on everything but the words (modules, signals, dish tags, links, update dates), calendar dates/chronology, append-only Google snapshots, Make a night of it and Pair it with targets, references into the recipe and profile collections, and the presence of every photograph under `public/images/date-spots/`. It also prints non-blocking warnings for reviews below nine earned internal links and for `favourite` drifting above one in four. Astro also validates individual records using the same schema. Consumers must render the Freshness Record, disclosure, and reasons; schema validation alone cannot prove they are visible.

`npm run test:contracts` exercises all four variants using explicit `[TEST ONLY]` tokens outside the content collection. Browser acceptance builds set `DATE_SPOT_SOURCE`, `CONTRIBUTOR_RECIPE_SOURCE` and `EXTENDED_PROFILE_SOURCE` to the files in `tests/fixtures/` (regenerated with `node tests/contracts/date-spot-fixtures.mjs`) to render a linked restaurant, bar, activity, chef page and recipe card; normal builds always load `src/content/`. The generic image exception is restricted to `test-only-` IDs. These values are test data, not reporting, human-authored translations, or publishable fixtures. No editorial content or photographs are generated by this implementation.

Review copy can optionally include `roomPhoto`, `meetChef` (or `meetTheBartender`), `makeANight`, and `atHome`, and Restaurant `whatToOrder` can include `waitersChoice`, `setMenu` and `strategy`. `atHome.recipeId` must name a published Contributor Recipe and `profileId` a published Extended Profile. Omitting any of these modules is valid; templates must not create filler for reporting that does not exist.

Before adding a real record, Victor must supply both complete human-authored versions and a genuine DMD-held photograph. The `authorship` and image `provenance` fields are required editorial attestations, not automated proof of origin. Human review must verify truthful reasons, accurate alt text, original reporting, appropriate occasion selection, and that an Activity does not duplicate a Restaurant or Bar. Validation cannot infer those facts from prose.

Contributor Recipes and Extended Profiles use parallel empty collections, `src/content/contributor-recipes.json` and `src/content/extended-profiles.json`, with their own executable contracts and Notion Story templates. They remain optional companions: a recipe must be supplied by an identified chef or bartender with a documented source; a profile must document original interview reporting and a companion Date Spot. See `notion/templates/` for their type-specific publication checklists.
