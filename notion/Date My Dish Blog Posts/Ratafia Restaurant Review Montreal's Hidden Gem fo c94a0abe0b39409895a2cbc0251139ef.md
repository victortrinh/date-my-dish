Ratafia is one of those Montreal restaurants that feels like a well-kept secret, but here's the insider tip: skip dinner and go straight for dessert. This intimate Mile End wine bar offers some of the city's best desserts paired with creative cocktails. It's the perfect post-meal date spot where you can linger over 2-3 dessert plates and discover unexpected flavor combinations in a cozy, conversation-friendly setting.

## The Vibe

Intimate and warm with that typical laid-back Montreal energy. The space is small and cozy, making it perfect for conversation without any stuffiness. Service is casual and friendly with no formal pretense here. Think of it as your neighborhood spot where you can settle in with desserts and drinks after dinner elsewhere.

**Lighting:** Warm and intimate

**Noise Level:** Quiet enough for deep conversation

**Best Seats:** Any table works in the small space. It's all intimate

---

## What to Order

**Must-Try:**

- **2-3 Dessert Plates** ($15-18 each, estimated): This is THE move. The rotating seasonal menu means you'll discover new flavor profiles each visit, from simple classics to out-of-the-box combinations.
- **Pair with cocktails**: Their cocktail list has creative twists that complement the desserts perfectly.

**For Sharing:** Order multiple desserts to share, great for trying different flavors and keeps the date interactive.

**Skip:** The savory menu. Mile End has too many better options for mains. Come here specifically for desserts.

---

## Drinks

Creative cocktail list with unique twists that pair beautifully with desserts. The wine selection is solid too, with natural wine options. This is a beverage-forward spot, so expect knowledgeable recommendations that match whatever seasonal desserts are on offer.

---

## The Real Cost

**Per Person:** $30-45 (2-3 dessert plates + 2 cocktails)

**Lighter Option:** $20-30 (1-2 desserts + drink)

**Worth It?** Absolutely. This is one of Montreal's best dessert experiences, and the intimate vibe makes it perfect for dates.

**Save Money:** Go for wine by the glass instead of cocktails, or share desserts.

---

## Booking & Timing

**Reserve:** Walk-ins welcome. You can also book via OpenTable if you prefer

**Best Time:** Post-dinner (after 8:30pm) when you're ready for dessert and drinks

**Walk-Ins:** Easy to get in, very accessible

## Date Type Fit

**First Date:** 4/5 - Perfect for a casual first date or as a second location after drinks/dinner elsewhere. Low pressure, great for talking.

**Anniversary:** 3/5 - Might be too casual for major milestones, but perfect for low-key celebrations with incredible desserts.

**Casual Night:** 5/5 - This is exactly what it's built for. Post-meal dessert dates with great conversation.

**Impressing:** 4/5 - The insider knowledge (going for desserts only) and flavor discovery impresses foodies without being pretentious.

---

## Bottom Line

**Overall: 8/10**

Ratafia shines as Montreal's go-to dessert date spot. Skip the mains, order 2-3 seasonal dessert plates with creative cocktails, and enjoy one of the city's most intimate settings for conversation. The rotating menu means every visit brings new flavor discoveries, simple one day and wildly inventive the next. It's casual, easy to book, and perfectly Mile End.

**Go Here If:** You want an intimate dessert date, love discovering new flavor combinations, or need a cozy spot for post-dinner drinks and sweets

**Skip If:** You're looking for a full dinner experience (go elsewhere in Mile End first, then come here)
