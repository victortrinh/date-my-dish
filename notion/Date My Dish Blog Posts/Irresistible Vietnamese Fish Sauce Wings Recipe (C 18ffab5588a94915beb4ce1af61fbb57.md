## Ingredients for Fish Sauce Wings

**For the Wings:**

- 500 g chicken wings
- 1 tsp black pepper (5 g)
- 2 tbsp vodka (30 ml)
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger (5 g)
- 1 tsp mushroom seasoning (5 g)
- 120 g potato starch (1 cup)
- Canola oil for frying

**For the Fish Sauce Caramel:**

- 6 tbsp sugar (75 g)
- 4 tbsp lime juice (60 ml)
- 4 tbsp fish sauce (60 ml)
- 2 Thai chilies, minced
- 2 tbsp minced garlic (about 20 g)
- 1 tsp fresh cilantro for garnish

**Ingredient Notes:**

Fish sauce is the backbone of this recipe. Use a good quality Vietnamese or Thai fish sauce. It smells strong in the bottle but transforms into something magical when cooked with sugar and lime.

Mushroom seasoning is a Vietnamese umami powder you can find at Asian grocery stores. If you can't find it, use a pinch of MSG or just salt.

Vodka in the marinade might seem weird, but it helps tenderize the chicken and evaporates during frying. You can substitute with rice wine or just leave it out.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

**Step 1: Marinate the Wings**

In a large bowl, combine the chicken wings with black pepper, grated ginger, vodka, and mushroom seasoning. Mix well with your hands. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, or up to 2 hours for more flavor.

**Step 2: Make the Fish Sauce Caramel**

In a small saucepan, combine the sugar, lime juice, fish sauce, and minced Thai chilies. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring until the sugar completely dissolves. Once it's liquid and slightly thickened, remove from heat and set aside. This is your flavor bomb.

**Step 3: Fry the Garlic**

In a small pan, heat about 3 tbsp of canola oil. Add the minced garlic and fry over medium heat until golden brown and crispy, about 2-3 minutes. Watch it carefully because garlic goes from golden to burnt quickly. Strain the garlic through a sieve, separating the crispy garlic from the oil. Set both aside.

**Step 4: Coat the Wings**

Put the potato starch on a plate. Take each marinated wing and roll it in the starch, making sure every part is coated. The starch creates that extra crispy texture.

**Step 5: First Fry**

Fill a heavy pot or deep fryer with canola oil to about 8 cm deep. Heat to 325°F (163°C). Carefully add the wings, working in batches so you don't overcrowd. Fry for 10 minutes. The wings will cook through but won't be super golden yet. Remove and place on a wire rack.

**Step 6: Rest the Wings**

Let the wings rest at room temperature for 5 minutes. This resting period lets the coating set and the interior stay juicy.

**Step 7: Second Fry**

Increase the oil temperature to 375°F (190°C). Fry the wings again for 4 minutes or until deep golden brown and extra crispy. Here's the science: the second fry at higher heat dehydrates the coating completely, making it shatteringly crisp.

**Step 8: Heat and Toss**

While the wings are on their second fry, reheat the fish sauce caramel until it's bubbling. As soon as the wings come out of the oil, transfer them to a large bowl. Pour the hot caramel over them, add the fried garlic, and toss vigorously for 30 seconds. You want every wing coated in that glossy sauce.

**Step 9: Garnish and Serve**

Transfer to a serving plate and garnish with fresh cilantro. Serve immediately while they're hot and crispy.

## Expert Tips for Perfect Fish Sauce Wings

**Don't Skip the Double Fry**

The two-stage frying is essential. The first fry cooks the wings, the second fry makes them crispy. Single-fried wings won't have the same texture.

**Toss While Everything is Hot**

The caramel needs to be hot and the wings need to be hot when you toss them together. This helps the sauce coat evenly and creates that lacquered finish.

**Control the Heat Level**

Thai chilies are spicy. If you're cooking for someone with a lower spice tolerance, use just one chili or remove the seeds.

**Work Quickly After the Second Fry**

The wings start to lose crispness once they're coated in sauce. Serve them right away for the best texture.

## FAQs About Fish Sauce Wings

**Can I bake these instead of frying?**

You can, but they won't be as crispy. If you bake them, use a wire rack on a baking sheet at 425°F for about 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway through.

**What if I don't like fish sauce?**

Fish sauce is essential to this recipe. The cooking process mellows its intensity and creates that signature umami flavor. If you really can't use it, try soy sauce mixed with a little anchovy paste, but it won't taste the same.

**How do I keep them crispy?**

Serve immediately. The sauce makes them less crispy over time, so they're best eaten right away.

**Can I use chicken drumettes or thighs instead?**

Yes, but adjust the cooking time. Drumettes need about 12-14 minutes on the first fry, thighs need 15 minutes.

**Why vodka in the marinade?**

Vodka helps tenderize the meat and adds moisture without adding flavor. It evaporates during cooking. You can skip it if you don't have any.
