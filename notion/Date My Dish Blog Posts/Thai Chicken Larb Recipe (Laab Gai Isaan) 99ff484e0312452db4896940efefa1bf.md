## Ingredients for Thai Chicken Larb

**For the Larb:**

- ¾ cup (180 ml) water
- ½ tsp sea salt
- 1 pound (450 g) ground chicken
- 3 tbsp Thai fish sauce
- 3 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 1 tbsp Thai cane sugar
- ½ to 2 tsp red chili powder
- 2 shallots, thinly sliced
- 3 stalks fresh sawtooth coriander, finely chopped
- 9 fresh mint leaves, coarsely sliced
- 15 fresh finger mint leaves, coarsely sliced
- 3 tbsp toasted rice powder

**For Serving:**

- 2 stalks cilantro, washed
- 3 yard-long beans, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 1 napa cabbage, cut in half
- ¼ small green cabbage, cut into 1-inch-thick wedges
- 1 English cucumber, cut into 1-inch-thick slices

**Ingredient Notes:**

Toasted rice powder is essential for authentic larb. You can make it by toasting sticky rice in a dry pan until golden, then grinding it. Find it at Asian markets.

Sawtooth coriander has a unique flavor. Regular cilantro works if you can't find it.

Finger mint (also called Vietnamese mint) has a peppery taste. Regular mint works in a pinch.

Thai fish sauce is saltier and funkier than Vietnamese. Use what you have, but Thai is traditional.

Ground chicken works best, but you can substitute ground turkey or pork.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

**Step 1: Cook the Chicken**

In a medium skillet over high heat, bring the water and salt to a boil. When the water is boiling, add the ground chicken and cook, stirring occasionally, for 3 minutes or until fully cooked. The chicken should no longer be pink. Drain the chicken and transfer to a medium bowl.

**Step 2: Season the Larb**

Add the fish sauce, lime juice, cane sugar, chili powder to taste (start with ½ teaspoon if you're sensitive to heat), shallots, sawtooth coriander, mint leaves, and finger mint leaves to the cooked chicken. Toss well to mix everything together. The chicken should be well coated with the dressing. Transfer to a plate.

**Step 3: Prepare the Vegetables**

Arrange the cilantro stalks, yard-long beans, napa cabbage halves, green cabbage wedges, and cucumber slices on a serving plate. These vegetables are for wrapping and eating with the larb.

**Step 4: Add the Toasted Rice Powder**

Just before serving, add the toasted rice powder to the chicken salad and stir well. The rice powder will absorb the dressing quickly, so don't add it too early or it will get soggy.

**Step 5: Serve**

Serve the larb immediately with the vegetables. To eat, wrap a spoonful of larb in cabbage leaves or lettuce, or eat it with the raw vegetables between bites for varying textures and flavors.

## Expert Tips for Perfect Thai Chicken Larb

**Add the Rice Powder Last**

Toasted rice powder absorbs liquid fast. Add it right before serving or it gets mushy.

**Adjust the Heat**

Start with less chili powder and add more to taste. You can always add heat but can't take it away.

**Use Fresh Lime Juice**

Bottled lime juice doesn't have the same bright, sour punch. Fresh is essential.

**Don't Overcook the Chicken**

Three minutes is enough. Overcooked ground chicken gets dry and rubbery.

**Serve with Crisp Vegetables**

The contrast between the warm larb and cold, crunchy vegetables is what makes this dish special.

## FAQs About Thai Chicken Larb

**Can I make this ahead?**

You can cook and season the chicken up to 1 day ahead. Add the rice powder and fresh herbs right before serving.

**What if I can't find toasted rice powder?**

Make your own by toasting uncooked sticky rice in a dry pan until golden brown, then grinding it in a spice grinder or mortar and pestle.

**Can I use chicken breast instead of ground chicken?**

Yes, but ground chicken is traditional and absorbs the seasonings better. If using breast, mince it very finely.

**How spicy is this?**

It depends on how much chili powder you use. Start with ½ teaspoon for mild heat, 2 teaspoons for authentic Isaan-level spice.

**Can I make this vegetarian?**

Substitute crumbled firm tofu or mushrooms for the chicken, and use soy sauce instead of fish sauce.
