## Ingredients for Thai Chicken Satay

**For the Chicken:**

- 1 tsp coriander seeds, toasted
- ¼ tsp white peppercorns
- 2 tbsp thinly sliced lemongrass
- 1 (2-inch) piece fresh turmeric, peeled and thinly sliced
- 1½ tsp sea salt, divided
- 2 tbsp water
- 2 tbsp evaporated milk
- 1 tbsp + 1½ tsp thin soy sauce
- 1 tbsp + 1½ tsp Thai oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 1 tsp tapioca starch
- 1½ pounds (675 g) skinless, boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
- ½ cup well-shaken coconut milk, for brushing

**For Serving:**

- Peanut sauce
- Fast pickled cucumber

**Ingredient Notes:**

Fresh turmeric is essential for authentic color and flavor. You can find it at Asian grocery stores. Don't substitute with powder.

Lemongrass should be thinly sliced from the tender white part only. The outer layers are too tough.

Chicken thighs stay juicier than breast meat on the grill. Don't substitute.

Thin soy sauce is lighter than regular soy sauce. Available at Asian markets.

Tapioca starch helps the marinade cling to the chicken. Cornstarch works in a pinch.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

**Step 1: Make the Marinade**

Using a stone mortar and pestle, grind the coriander seeds and white peppercorns to a fine powder. Add the lemongrass, turmeric, and 1 teaspoon of the salt and pound to a smooth paste. This takes about 5 minutes of pounding. Transfer to a large bowl. Add the water, evaporated milk, thin soy sauce, oyster sauce, condensed milk, vegetable oil, curry powder, tapioca starch, and the remaining ½ teaspoon salt. Stir well to combine.

**Step 2: Marinate the Chicken**

Add the chicken pieces to the marinade and toss to coat well. Make sure every piece is fully coated. Cover with plastic wrap and place in the fridge for at least 1 hour to marinate. For best results, marinate up to 4 hours.

**Step 3: Soak the Skewers**

Soak 30 (6-inch) bamboo skewers in cold water for at least 30 minutes. This prevents them from burning on the grill.

**Step 4: Thread the Chicken**

Thread the marinated chicken onto one half of each skewer, leaving the other half as a handle. Use 2-3 pieces per skewer.

**Step 5: Prepare the Grill**

Prepare the grill for direct cooking over high heat. Brush the grill grates with the coconut milk and let sit for a couple of minutes. This will prevent the chicken from sticking to the grill.

**Step 6: Grill the Satay**

Place the chicken skewers on the grill and cook until they begin to char, 8 to 10 minutes. Turn and brush the other side with coconut milk when the chicken starts to dry out. Cook for another 6 to 8 minutes, until a nice char appears on the meat and the chicken is cooked through. The internal temperature should reach 165°F.

**Step 7: Serve**

Serve immediately with peanut sauce and fast pickled cucumber.

## Expert Tips for Perfect Thai Chicken Satay

**Don't Skip the Mortar and Pestle**

Pounding the aromatics releases their oils and creates a paste that clings to the chicken. A food processor won't give you the same texture.

**Brush the Grill with Coconut Milk**

This Thai trick prevents sticking and adds subtle sweetness. Don't skip this step.

**Use Fresh Turmeric**

Powdered turmeric won't give you the same color or flavor. Fresh is essential for authentic satay.

**Don't Overcrowd the Grill**

Leave space between skewers so they char properly. Work in batches if needed.

**Make Extra Marinade**

This marinade is so good you'll want to use it on other dishes. Double the batch and freeze half.

## FAQs About Thai Chicken Satay

**Can I use chicken breast instead of thighs?**

You can, but thighs stay juicier on the grill. If using breast, watch carefully to avoid overcooking.

**Can I make this in the oven?**

Yes. Broil on high for 8-10 minutes per side, but you'll miss the smoky char from the grill.

**How long can I marinate the chicken?**

Minimum 1 hour, maximum 4 hours. Longer than that and the chicken gets mushy from the acids.

**Can I make the marinade ahead?**

Yes. The paste keeps in the fridge for up to 3 days. Add the chicken when ready to marinate.

**What if I don't have fresh turmeric?**

Fresh is best, but you can substitute 1 teaspoon of turmeric powder in a pinch. The color won't be as vibrant.
